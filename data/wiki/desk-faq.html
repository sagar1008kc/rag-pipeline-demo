Policy desk FAQ

Short answers for the internal assistant. Vault PDFs win if this page disagrees.

How do I classify a client TIN?

A taxpayer identification number is Confidential. Do not paste it into unsanctioned chat tools. See POL-PII-2026.

When is a PII leak SEV-1?

When a full TIN or full account number is confirmed in an unsanctioned tool, or when an ethical wall is breached. Page the CISO on-call within 15 minutes. See the severity matrix CSV and the incident PDF.

How long do we keep retrieval audit logs?

Seven years in the audit lake. Assistant answers are not a system of record.
